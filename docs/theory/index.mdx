These pages explain the physics, mathematics, and algorithms that Nucleide
implements. They are written for analysts and contributors who want to
understand *why* the code behaves the way it does, not just *how* to call it.

## When to read each page

| Page | Read if you are... |
| --- | --- |
| [Depletion](depletion.mdx) | Solving burnup chains and want to understand the Bateman equation, transmutation matrices, and CRAM. |
| [Enrichment cascades](enrichment.mdx) | Configuring multicomponent enrichment cascades and need the MARC/SWU derivation. |
| [Variance reduction](variance-reduction.mdx) | Generating MAGIC weight windows or sampling mesh-based sources. |
| [Nuclear data](nuclear-data.mdx) | Looking up nuclide IDs, masses, half-lives, or converting between code-name dialects. |
| [Point kinetics](kinetics.mdx) | Running prescribed-reactivity transients and want the PKE system, the inhour relation, and the prompt-jump approximation. |
| [Tritium transport](tritium.mdx) | Running 1D diffusion-trapping permeation and want the McNabb–Foster system, the surface taxonomy, and the closed-form permeation checks. |
| [Gamma-ray spectroscopy](spectroscopy.mdx) | Smoothing spectra, counting peaks, calibrating energy/efficiency, X-ray lines, SDEF decay-source cards, and the SPE readers. |
| [UQ-lite sampling](uq-sampling.mdx) | Seeded MVN, log-normal, and LHS sampling over caller-supplied covariance blocks, SANDY-compatible estimators, and the decay and fission-yield perturbation consumers. |
| [Damage metrics](damage-metrics.mdx) | Folding multigroup fluxes into NRT-dpa and arc-dpa, gas production, and He/dpa ratios, with uncertainty propagation through the fold and coil lifetime bookkeeping. |
| [Fusion neutron sources](fusion-sources.mdx) | Building D-D/D-T ring, point, lattice, and parametric tokamak sources — including fuel mixtures, the hot-tail shape, and ECRH accessibility — and want the Ballabio spectra, Bosch–Hale reactivity, and the Miller-geometry map. |
| [Neutron spectrum unfolding](unfolding.mdx) | Recovering a spectrum from activation detector measurements with SAND-II, least-squares, or GRAVEL adjustment, including the IRDFF response pack. |
| [Clearance screening](clearance-screening.mdx) | Classifying activation inventories against the EU or Spanish clearance-limit tables, plus the S1+S2 activity and decay-heat totals. |
| [TBR and blanket bookkeeping](blanket.mdx) | Raw TBR from caller tallies, port-penalty haircuts, energy multiplication, burn rate, and breeding margins. |
| [Equilibrium data readers](equilibrium.mdx) | Classic-netCDF wout reading, the INDATA grammar, flux-surface Jacobians, and wall-load mapping. |

## Structure of a theory page

Each page follows the same pattern:

1. **Overview** — what problem the method solves.
2. **Mathematical details** — the governing equations and symbols.
3. **Algorithm** — how Nucleide turns the math into code.
4. **Worked example** — a small hand-verified calculation.
5. **Assumptions and limitations** — explicit list of what is and is not
   handled.
6. **Related work beyond Nucleide** — adjacent methods in PyNE, OpenMC, and
   the literature that Nucleide does not (yet) implement.
7. **References** — sources are cited inline at the claims they back, with
   the full bibliography at the end of the page.
