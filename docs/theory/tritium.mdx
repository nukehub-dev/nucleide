Nucleide covers activation (ALARA/FISPACT/R2S) but has nothing on tritium
inventory and permeation in fusion materials. This page pins the **spec-phase
equation set** for a scoped 1D kernel: Fickian diffusion of a mobile
population `c_m` coupled to `N` McNabb–Foster trap species<Citation id="mcnabb-1963" />, with
caller-supplied temperature and surface laws. The `nucleide-tritium` kernel
implements this equation set; the T1–T2/G1–G6 labels below are the
analytic-gate contract its tests replay, mirroring the E1–E4/O-gate pattern
of the point-kinetics page.
Equations are textbook (Crank; McNabb–Foster; Oriani;
Shewmon)<Citation id="crank-1975" /><Citation id="mcnabb-1963" />
<Citation id="oriani-1970" /><Citation id="shewmon-2016" /> —
no simulator source was read to produce them.

## Overview

A 1D slab `0 ≤ x ≤ L` holds mobile tritium `c_m(x, t)` plus trapped
populations `c_{t,j}(x, t)` for trap species `j = 1..N` (vacancies,
dislocations, grain boundaries — distinguished only by their rate data).
Tritium moves by Fickian diffusion with a caller-supplied diffusivity `D(T)`,
parks in traps with temperature-dependent trapping/detrapping rates, and
enters or leaves through surface laws (fixed concentration, Sieverts
equilibrium, recombination flux, or symmetry). Temperature is a
caller-supplied profile `T(x, t)`; there is no heat solve in v1. Series
stacks of slabs — multi-layer walls such as W/Cu/CuCrZr first walls —
extend the same equations layer-by-layer with Sieverts interface
conditions (L1) and the series-resistance steady state (L2).

## Mathematical details

### T1 — Mobile balance

$$
\frac{\partial c_m}{\partial t}
= \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\!\left(D(T)\,\frac{\partial c_m}{\partial x}\right)
- \sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\partial c_{t,j}}{\partial t} + S(x, t).
\tag{T1}
$$

`S` is a caller-supplied volumetric source (mol/m³/s); initial profiles
`c_m(x, 0)` and `c_{t,j}(x, 0)` are caller-supplied (default zero).

### T2 — McNabb–Foster trap kinetics

Per trap species with site density `N_j` (mol/m³):

$$
\frac{\partial c_{t,j}}{\partial t}
= k_j(T)\,c_m\,(N_j - c_{t,j}) - p_j(T)\,c_{t,j}.
\tag{T2}
$$

At equilibrium (`∂/∂t = 0`) each species sits on its Langmuir isotherm

$$
c_{t,j} = \frac{N_j K_j c_m}{1 + K_j c_m},
\qquad K_j = k_j / p_j,
\tag{T2-eq}
$$

with occupancy `θ_j = K_j c_m / (1 + K_j c_m)`.

### Caller-supplied Arrhenius stance

The kernel ships **no material property tables** — the same stance as
kinetics delayed data. Every coefficient is caller data, constant or
Arrhenius in the caller-supplied temperature:

$$
D(T) = D_0 e^{-E_D/RT}, \qquad
k_j(T) = k_{j0} e^{-E_{kj}/RT}, \qquad
p_j(T) = p_{j0} e^{-E_{pj}/RT}, \qquad
K_S(T) = K_{S0} e^{-E_S/RT}.
\tag{T-Arr}
$$

Trap site densities `N_j` are static in v1 (no damage-created intrinsic
trap evolution).

### Boundary-condition taxonomy

At each surface (`n` the outward normal, `J = −D∂c_m/∂n` the outward flux)<Citation id="shewmon-2016" />:

- **Dirichlet**: `c_m = c̄` (prescribed surface concentration).
- **Sieverts**: `c_m = K_S(T)√p` (diatomic-gas equilibrium at gas pressure
  `p`; the linear Henry variant `c_m = K_H p` is a named alternative).
- **Recombination**: `J = K_r(T)\,c_m²` (surface recombination to molecules;
  nonlinear Robin condition).
- **ZeroFlux**: `J = 0` (symmetry midplane or impermeable wall).

### G1 — Steady linear profile

Steady trap-free T1 with constant `D`, `S = 0`, and Dirichlet ends
`c(0) = c0`, `c(L) = cL` gives `d²c/dx² = 0`, hence

$$
c(x) = c_0 + (c_L - c_0)\,x/L, \qquad
J_{ss} = D\,(c_0 - c_L)/L, \qquad
I = (c_0 + c_L)\,L/2.
\tag{G1}
$$

`I` is the mobile inventory per unit area. Pinned in
`fixtures/tritium/g1_steady.json` (algebraic, tolerance `1e-12`).

### G2 — Permeation transient and time lag

With `c(0,t) = c0`, `c(L,t) = 0`, `c(x,0) = 0`, splitting off the G1 steady
profile leaves a homogeneous heat equation solved by separation of variables
(eigenfunctions `sin(nπx/L)`, eigenvalues `Dn²π²/L²`)<Citation id="crank-1975" />. The outlet flux is

$$
\frac{J(L,t)}{J_{ss}}
= 1 + 2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n
e^{-D n^2 \pi^2 t / L^2}.
\tag{G2}
$$

Cumulative permeation `Q(t) = ∫₀ᵗ J dt` asymptotes to `J_ss(t − t_lag)` with

$$
t_{lag} = L^2 / 6D,
\tag{G2-lag}
$$

the breakthrough-curve intercept. Pinned in
`fixtures/tritium/g2_timelag.json` at seven log-spaced times (series cut at
`|term| < 1e-18`; solver replay tolerance `1e-6`).

### G3a — Low-occupancy (Oriani) limit

For `K_j c_m ≪ 1`, (T2-eq) linearizes to `c_{t,j} ≈ N_jK_jc_m`<Citation id="oriani-1970" />; under local
equilibrium T1+T2 collapse to `(1 + ΣN_jK_j)∂c_m/∂t = D∇²c_m`, i.e.

$$
D_{eff} = D / (1 + KN) \quad \text{(single trap)}.
\tag{G3a}
$$

Pinned in `fixtures/tritium/g3a_oriani.json` (`KN = 3`, `D_eff = D/4`;
algebraic, tolerance `1e-12`).

### G3b — Saturated limit

For `K_j c_m ≫ 1`, `c_{t,j} → N_j`: traps sit full, the mobile steady state
is G1, and the trapped load follows the isotherm pointwise. Pinned in
`fixtures/tritium/g3b_saturated.json` with the exact trapped inventory
`2L(1 − ln 2)` (algebraic, tolerance `1e-12`) — evaluated at `Kc = 1` at
`x = 0` (occupancy 0.5 falling to 0), so the gate pins the full-isotherm
integral with the saturated asymptote `N·L` as its limiting interpretation.

### G3c — Irreversible limit

For `p_j → 0` at fixed mobile `c_m`, (T2) integrates directly (with
`c_t(0) = 0`):

$$
c_t(t) = N\,(1 - e^{-k c_m t}).
\tag{G3c}
$$

Pinned in `fixtures/tritium/g3c_irreversible.json` at seven times
(algebraic, tolerance `1e-12`).

### G4 — Sieverts steady state

Sieverts ends `c(0) = K_S√p₁`, `c(L) = K_S√p₂` reduce the trap-free steady
state to G1 between those concentrations, with permeability `Φ = D·K_S`:

$$
J = \Phi\,(\sqrt{p_1} - \sqrt{p_2}) / L.
\tag{G4}
$$

Pinned in `fixtures/tritium/g4_sieverts.json` (algebraic, tolerance
`1e-12`).

### G5 — Recombination steady state

With a recombination outlet the steady problem is nonlinear, but the
mobile profile stays affine in the recombination face values (the steady
system is linear for frozen faces), so the kernel pins each face-adjacent
response with unit-perturbation Thomas solves: one end closes in closed
form from `K_r cf² + g(1−b) cf − g·a = 0`, two ends by Newton iteration
with the analytic Jacobian. **G5a** (Dirichlet + recombination,
trap-free, `S = 0`) asserts the linear `(c0, c_s)` profile, flux, and
inventory at `1e-12`; **G5b** asserts the `K_r → ∞` recovery of the G1
Dirichlet end. The recombination *transient* has no such algebraic
oracle and closes per step instead (G6).

### G6 — Recombination transient

The recombination *transient* admits **no closed form**. The face law
`K_r cf² = g(c_adj − cf)` makes every implicit θ-step nonlinear, and the
face quadratic couples to the time derivative through the step matrix:
the separation-of-variables trick behind G2 needs a linear problem, and
the frozen-face affine reduction behind G5 needs no time derivative, so
neither extends. The kernel instead reuses the G5 affine face-response
construction *inside* each θ-step: for frozen traps the step is linear
in the mobile profile with the face values as sources (`M c = r + Q f`),
so one base Thomas solve plus one sensitivity column `P = M⁻¹Q` per
recombination end (rebuilt only when `dt` changes) makes the profile
affine in the faces, `c = base + P f`. Trap-free steps close each face
in closed form (one end) or by Newton iteration with the analytic
Jacobian (two ends, warm-started from the previous step); steps with
traps fuse the face iterate into the trap-Picard loop, sharing
`rtol`/`atol`. A face Newton that exhausts its cap fails the step with
the existing not-converged error (hard fail, no `dt` retry).

Because no algebraic oracle exists, the G6a–G6d gates are asymptotic and
self-convergence — honestly weaker than the G1–G5 algebraic gates:

- **G6a (long-time asymptote)**: Dirichlet + recombination from a clean
  slab lands on the G5a steady state: `1e-6` relative on the outlet flux,
  the flux balance `|F_L + F_R|`, and the G5a linear profile at `1e-9`,
  at `t = 60·t_lag ≈ 44·τ₁` — `τ₁ = L²/(D y₁²)` the slowest mode of the
  problem linearized about the steady face value (`y₁` the first positive
  root of `y·cos y + (2K_r c_s L/D)·sin y = 0`), so the slowest mode has
  decayed to `e⁻⁴⁴` and only the asymptote is gated.
- **G6b (K_r continuity)**: `K_r → ∞` recovers the G2 Dirichlet
  transient (outlet-flux series at `1e-6`), matching the G5b steady
  limit; `K_r → 0` recovers the zero-flux transient (profile at `1e-5`
  absolute against the leading-order `K_r c_f² t` leak).
- **G6c (mass balance)**: recombination leaks, so the discrete invariant
  `Iᵏ − Iᵏ⁻¹ = dt·[θRᵏ + (1−θ)Rᵏ⁻¹]` with `R = −(F_L + F_R)` closes
  against the reported `K_r cf²` faces to roundoff (`1e-12` relative).
- **G6d (self-convergence)**: `dt`-halving on a fixed recombination case
  shows the θ-method order on two successive halvings — ≈ 2 for
  Crank–Nicolson (pinned band 1.5–2.5), ≈ 1 for backward Euler (pinned
  band 0.7–1.3). The band is measured where truncation error dominates:
  the `t = 0` corner kink excites Crank–Nicolson fine-mode ringing that
  decays only as `|g| → 1` on modes with `λ·dt ≫ 2`, so coarse-`dt`
  errors at late time are ringing, not truncation.
- **G6e (independent cross-check)**: self-convergence cannot catch a
  spatial-discretization or face-formula bug that is consistent across
  refinements, so G6e compares the mid-transient trajectory at
  `t = 0.5/1/2·t_lag` against an independent solver — the method of
  lines with second-order central differences on a node-centred grid
  (ghost-node recombination end) and a hand-rolled explicit RK4 — that
  shares no discretization, face closure, or integrator with the kernel.
  The trap-free G6a slab plus a trap-coupled variant agree with the
  production transient at `1.5e-4` relative on the mobile-profile
  max-norm (`6e-4` on the trapped profile; outlet flux within `5e-3`
  relative or `1e-3·J_ss` absolute), ≈5x headroom over the measured
  cross-method error.

Positivity under the face clamp (`cf ≥ 0`) is asserted for one- and
two-recombination-end transients on a coarse grid.

### Multi-layer series stacks

A `LayerStack` chains `N ≥ 1` slabs — e.g. a W/Cu/CuCrZr first-wall stack —
each carrying (T1–T2) with its own coefficients `D_k`, `K_{S,k}`, trap
species, temperature, and source. Internal interfaces close through local
equilibrium (v1: Sieverts only): the Sieverts potential `u = c_m / K_S`
\[Pa¹ᐟ²\] and the flux `J = −D ∂c_m/∂x` are continuous,

$$
u\big|_{k^-} = u\big|_{k^+}, \qquad
D_k\,\frac{\partial c_m}{\partial x}\bigg|_{k^-}
= D_{k+1}\,\frac{\partial c_m}{\partial x}\bigg|_{k^+}.
\tag{L1}
$$

The Henry and recombination interface laws exist in the taxonomy but are
loud unsupported-interface errors in v1 — never a silent pass. The steady
state is the series-resistance network over the per-layer permeabilities
`Φ_k = D_k K_{S,k}`: `u(x)` is piecewise linear with constant flux

$$
J = \frac{u(0) - u(L)}{\sum_k L_k / \Phi_k}.
\tag{L2}
$$

The discrete form keeps the mobile concentration as the unknown and writes
every interior face flux as `J_f = (c_i/K_i − c_{i+1}/K_{i+1}) / R_f` with
face resistance `R_f = dx_k/(2Φ_k) + dx_{k+1}/(2Φ_{k+1})` — linear in the
neighbouring cell values, so the Sieverts condition folds directly into the
tridiagonal θ-step matrix (the spike outcome: no interface Newton is needed,
unlike the nonlinear recombination faces of G5/G6). Within one uniform
layer it reduces to the landed arithmetic-mean face. Outer ends reuse the
landed surface taxonomy unchanged, including recombination ends through the
same face-response construction. A one-layer stack dispatches to the landed
single-slab kernel and reproduces it exactly (the G7c anchor).

### G7 — Multi-layer steady state

The layered steady system is linear (for frozen recombination faces), so
the algebraic gates carry over at G5-style tolerances:

- **G7a (series resistance)**: a 2-layer stack with contrasting `D` and
  `K_S` matches the (L2) closed form at `1e-12` relative on the fluxes, and
  every interior face — including the material interface — carries the same
  flux to roundoff (with a `1e-18` absolute floor, since the fluxes are
  ~1e-7 and plain relative roundoff would be unmeetable); the discrete
  profile is the exact piecewise-linear `u(x)` at the nodes.
- **G7b (N-layer continuity)**: a 3-layer stack holds interface
  flux continuity and the half-cell-corrected interface potentials to
  roundoff against the closed form.
- **G7c (single-layer recovery)**: a 1-layer stack reproduces the landed
  kernel field-by-field, exactly (the layered entry dispatches to it).
- **G7d (transparent interface)**: a 2-layer stack with identical `D` and
  `K_S` in both layers is a plain slab; the layered path itself (not the
  dispatch) matches the landed kernel to roundoff.
- **G7e (recombination outer end)**: the layered face-response construction
  closes a recombination outlet against the same series resistance; the
  flux matches the closed form from `J = K_r (K_{S,n} u_L)^2` with
  `u(0) − u(L) = J·R` at `1e-12` relative.

### G8 — Multi-layer transient

The transient reuses the landed θ-stepper with the interface fluxes inside
the step matrix; recombination outer ends close per step through the
face-response construction on the layered matrix. No algebraic transient
oracle exists, so the gates mirror G6:

- **G8a (self-convergence)**: `dt`-halving on the 2-layer stack shows the
  θ-method order on two successive halvings — Crank–Nicolson in the band
  1.5–2.5, backward Euler in 0.7–1.3 — measured in the same resolved band as
  G6d (the stack's finest-mode scale and lag time set it; measured orders
  sit at ≈2.0 and ≈1.0).
- **G8b (long-time asymptote)**: the 2-layer transient from a clean slab
  lands on the layered steady state at `t = 60·max_k(L_k²/6D_k)`, within
  `1e-6` relative on the outlet flux and `1e-9` on the profile, for both a
  Dirichlet and a recombination outlet.
- **G8c (mass balance)**: with one θ-step per output interval the discrete
  balance `Iᵏ − Iᵏ⁻¹ = dt·[θRᵏ + (1−θ)Rᵏ⁻¹]`, `R = −(F_L + F_R)`, closes
  against the reported boundary fluxes to roundoff — internal interface
  fluxes cancel in the sum, so the landed G6c identity carries over.

Positivity is asserted on the layered transient with different trap species
per layer (per-cell trap counts follow the owning layer), and the loud
error surface (non-Sieverts interfaces, malformed stacks and states) is
gated in-crate.

### Invariants

- **Positivity**: nonneg initial data, boundary data, and source imply
  `c_m, c_{t,j} ≥ 0` (maximum-principle consequence). The future discrete
  scheme must preserve it.
- **Mass conservation**: `d/dt[I_m + ΣI_{t,j}] = J(0,t) − J(L,t) + ∫S dx`
  with `J = −D∂c_m/∂x`. The future scheme must satisfy it to roundoff.

## Algorithm

The discretization is a 1D cell-centred finite-volume grid with implicit
θ-stepping in the kinetics `solve.rs` pattern (diffusion implicit, traps
via the exact per-cell backward-Euler map with Picard coupling to
rtol/atol). Each implicit step is a tridiagonal solve through the shared
`linalg::tridiag` Thomas module the kernel landing added behind the
workspace facade. Gate-to-test mapping: G1/G3a/G3b/G3c/G4 replay as
algebraic assertions (`1e-12`), G2 as a transient solve (`1e-6`), G5 as
the recombination steady-state face construction (G5a algebraic at
`1e-12`, G5b recovery at `1e-6`), G6 as the recombination transient
(G6a–G6d asymptotic + self-convergence gates with in-test closed forms,
plus the G6e independent method-of-lines cross-check), G7/G8 as the
multi-layer gates (series-resistance closed forms at `1e-12` with
roundoff-level interface continuity pins; transient asymptote +
self-convergence + mass balance), plus positivity and mass-balance
invariant checks.

## Worked example: G1 slab

With `D = 1e-9 m²/s`, `L = 1e-3 m`, `c0 = 1.0`, `cL = 0.0 mol/m³`, (G1)
gives `c = [1.0, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, 0.0]` at `x/L = [0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1]`,
`J_ss = 1e-6 mol/m²/s`, and `I = 5e-4 mol/m²`. The same slab switched to
the G2 transient breaks through at `t_lag = L²/6D ≈ 166.67 s`: the outlet
flux is still `≈2.3e-3 J_ss` at `t = 31.6 s` and reaches `0.9999 J_ss` by
`t = 1000 s`, exactly the `fixtures/tritium/g2_timelag.json` vector.

## Assumptions and limitations

- **1D only**: multi-D FEM and heat coupling are explicitly out — that is
  FESTIM's reason to exist, and FESTIM is never a dependency. Heterogeneous
  *series* stacks in 1D (multi-layer, Sieverts interfaces) are in scope
  (G7/G8); in-plane multi-material geometry is not.
- **Prescribed temperature**: `T(x, t)` is caller data; no heat solve.
- **Caller-supplied properties**: diffusivities, solubilities, trapping
  rates, and surface constants ship as caller data; no vendored tables.
- **Sieverts interfaces only**: internal interface conditions are Sieverts
  (`c/K_S` continuous, flux continuous) in v1; Henry/recombination interface
  laws are loud errors, and trap-species discontinuities across interfaces
  enter only through caller data.
- **Single mobile species**: tritium only; H/D co-diffusion and isotope
  exchange are out.
- **Static traps**: `N_j` fixed; damage-driven intrinsic trap creation is
  deferred to a later design (the T2 form already covers both).
- **Equilibrium surfaces**: Dirichlet/Sieverts ends assume fast surface
  kinetics; finite-rate surface processes enter only via recombination.
- **G6 transient gates**: the recombination steady state is pinned
  (G5a/G5b); the recombination transient closes per θ-step (G6a–G6d
  asymptotic + self-convergence gates, plus the G6e independent
  method-of-lines cross-check) — no closed-form transient oracle exists,
  and none is claimed.
- **Thin facades only**: `nucleide.tritium` (Python) and
  `tritiumBreakthrough` (WASM) expose the landed kernel; no new physics
  past T1–T2/G1–G8.

## Validation

- **Analytic replay** (landed): Rust fixture tests plus
  `tests/test_tritium.py` assert the G1–G4 fixtures at the pinned
  tolerances and replay G5/G6 and the multi-layer G7/G8 gates as in-test
  closed forms (synthetic stacks with hand-derived series-resistance
  oracles; the multi-layer goldens live in unit tests, not
  `fixtures/tritium/`), following the kinetics oracle pattern. No
  `validation/*_vs_*.py` script exists — there is no external oracle to
  wire, so none was added.
- **No external probe**: a FESTIM cross-check, if
  ever attempted, stays a loud-SKIP probe. Do **not** add FESTIM/FEniCS to
  the validation container: the conda weight is intolerable for an
  optional nicety, and the analytic gates stand alone.
- `validation/` is untouched by this spec change.
