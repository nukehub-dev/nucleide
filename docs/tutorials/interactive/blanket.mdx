A breeding blanket must make at least as much tritium as the plasma burns.
The **tritium breeding ratio (TBR)** is bred tritons per source neutron;
ports and penetrations shave it down multiplicatively, and the remainder
minus one is the breeding margin. Combined with the fusion power, the same
inputs give the daily tritium burn and the net surplus (or deficit).

The demo below runs that bookkeeping over editable caller tallies — all
numbers are synthetic. No transport happens here; the tallies come from your
neutronics calculation.
