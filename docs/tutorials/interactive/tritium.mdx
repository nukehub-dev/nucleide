A 1D slab $0 \le x \le L$ holds mobile tritium $c_m(x, t)$ with Fickian
diffusion at a caller-supplied diffusivity $D$ (T1, trap-free here):

$$
\frac{\partial c_m}{\partial t}
= D\,\frac{\partial^2 c_m}{\partial x^2}.
\tag{T1}
$$

The demo below solves the permeation setup $c(0, t) = c_0$, $c(L, t) = 0$,
$c(x, 0) = 0$ with the crate's implicit finite-volume stepper and plots the
normalized outlet flux $J(L, t)/J_{ss}$ (G2):

$$
\frac{J(L,t)}{J_{ss}}
= 1 + 2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n
e^{-D n^2 \pi^2 t / L^2}.
\tag{G2}
$$

The dashed marker sits at the time lag $t_{\mathrm{lag}} = L^2/6D$ (G2-lag),
the intercept of the cumulative-permeation asymptote. The default inputs
replay `fixtures/tritium/g2_timelag.json` ($D = 10^{-9}\ \mathrm{m}^2/\mathrm{s}$,
$L = 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m}$, $c_0 = 1.0\ \mathrm{mol}/\mathrm{m}^3$,
$t_{\mathrm{lag}} \approx 166.67\ \mathrm{s}$). All inputs are validated
inline (finite, with $L > 0$, $D > 0$, $c_0 \ge 0$, $t_{\mathrm{end}} > 0$)
before any solve runs.
