Prescribed-reactivity point kinetics tracks the neutron population $n(t)$
and $G$ delayed-neutron precursor groups $C_i(t)$ under a caller-supplied
reactivity schedule $\rho(t)$ in Δk (E1):

$$
\frac{dn}{dt} = \frac{\rho - \beta}{\Lambda}\,n + \sum_i \lambda_i C_i,
\qquad
\frac{dC_i}{dt} = \frac{\beta_i}{\Lambda}\,n - \lambda_i C_i.
\tag{E1}
$$

Precursors start at the equilibrium ratio (E2):

$$
C_i(0) = \frac{\beta_i}{\lambda_i \Lambda}\,n_0,
\tag{E2}
$$

and a step $\rho^- \to \rho^+$ carries the prompt-jump estimate (E4):

$$
n^+ = n^-\,\frac{\beta - \rho^-}{\beta - \rho^+},
\qquad \rho^+ < \beta.
\tag{E4}
$$

## Step transient

The demo below solves one step-reactivity transient with the crate's
stiff-aware implicit integrator and reports the (E4) jump next to the
$n(t)$ curve. Delayed data are caller-supplied: the **One-group preset**
replays `fixtures/kinetics/step_oracle.json` ($\beta = 0.0065$,
$\lambda = 0.08\ \mathrm{s}^{-1}$, $\Lambda = 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{s}$, step
$0 \to 0.002\ \Delta k$ at $t = 1\ \mathrm{s}$), and the **Six-group
preset** uses the hand-picked synthetic set from
`fixtures/kinetics/inhour_check.json`. All inputs are validated inline
(finite, with $0 \le t_{\mathrm{step}} \lt t_{\mathrm{end}}$ and
$n_0 \ge 0$) before any solve runs. When $\rho^+ \ge \beta$ the (E4)
formula has no solution and the readout says so while the solver still
runs.

## Inhour and prompt-jump scalars

The **Inhour analysis** button below the transient runs the E3–E4 leftovers
over the current delayed data: `inhourRho` at ω = 0.05 1/s, `stablePeriod`
at ρ = 0.002 Δk, and the standalone `promptJump` factor.
