A burning deuterium–tritium (or deuterium–deuterium) plasma is a neutron
source with a thermal spectrum: the birth energy of each neutron follows a
Gaussian whose width grows with the square root of the ion temperature —
the Brysk scaling, refined by the closed-form fits of **Ballabio et al.,
Nucl. Fusion 38 (1998) 1723**. At the 14.1 MeV D-T line the broadening is
small at reactor temperatures but never exactly zero.

The demo below samples a tokamak midplane ring source (fixed major radius
and height, uniform azimuth, isotropic directions) with a seeded generator,
histograms the sampled birth energies, and renders the same configuration as
MCNP `SDEF` and Serpent `src` cards that transport codes can read directly.
Identical inputs always reproduce the identical particle stream.
