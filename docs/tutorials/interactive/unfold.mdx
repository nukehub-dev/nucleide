Foil-activation spectrometry measures a handful of reaction rates — each
detector is sensitive to a different energy band — and the spectrum behind
those rates must be reconstructed. **SAND-II** (McElroy et al., AFWL-TR-67-41,
1967) does this iteratively: it folds the current spectrum guess through the
response matrix, compares the calculated rates against the measured ones, and
multiplies each energy group by a weighted geometric mean of the correction
factors, so the spectrum stays positive and a guess that already fits is left
unchanged.

The demo below ships a small determined synthetic problem — four detectors,
six energy groups, Gaussian response bumps — with editable measured rates.
Press **Unfold spectrum** to watch the adjusted spectrum against the flat
guess, the iteration count, and how well the final fold reproduces the rates
you entered. The response values are synthetic round numbers, not evaluated
detector data.
