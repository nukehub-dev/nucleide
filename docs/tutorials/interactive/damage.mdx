Neutron bombardment displaces atoms from their lattice sites and transmutes
them into gases such as helium and hydrogen. Two closed-form damage models
convert a primary knock-on atom (PKA) energy into a displacement count: the
classic **NRT model** (Norgett, Robinson & Torrens, 1975) and the
**arc-dpa correction** (Nordlund et al., 2018), which multiplies the NRT
result by an efficiency below one fitted to molecular-dynamics cascades.
Gas production is counted the same way with helium-production cross sections.

The demo below folds a three-group flux with caller-supplied response cross
sections, exactly as a reactor dosimetry pipeline would: each group
contributes `flux × response`, and the sum scales with exposure time. All
numbers are synthetic round values — no evaluated nuclear data is bundled.
The arc-efficiency chart shows the Nordlund correction ξ falling with PKA
energy for the material constants you set.
